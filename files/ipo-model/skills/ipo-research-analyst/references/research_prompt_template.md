# Prompt to paste into other AI models

Research the Indian mainboard IPO of **{COMPANY}** as of **{DATE}**. Use web search. Cite a source URL for every number. If you cannot verify a number, write "NOT FOUND" instead of estimating.

Return a report with these sections:

1. **Issue details:** open/close/listing dates, price band (upper), issue size (INR crore), fresh issue vs offer-for-sale split (%), lot size, exchange.
2. **Subscription (final overall multiples, or latest if still open, stating the date):** QIB, NII/HNI, retail, total.
3. **Grey market premium:** latest GMP as a % of issue price, with date and source.
4. **Valuation:** issue P/E, median P/E of 3-5 listed peers (name them), P/B if relevant.
5. **Financials (last 3 years):** revenue and growth %, profit after tax and margin %, ROE, ROCE, debt-to-equity.
6. **Market context:** Nifty 50 return in the 30 days before the issue opened, India VIX on the closing date, listing gains of the last 5 mainboard IPOs.
7. **Business and risks:** what the company does, use of proceeds, promoter holding and pledges, litigation, customer or supplier concentration, anchor investors, lead managers.
8. **Your own view:** positives, negatives, and whether listing gain looks likely. Label it clearly as opinion.

Format numbers as plain numbers (no currency symbols in tables). End with a list of anything you could not verify.
