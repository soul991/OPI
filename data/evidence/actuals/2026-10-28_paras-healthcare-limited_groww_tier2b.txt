<h2><span style="font-weight: 400;">About Paras Healthcare Limited</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Established in 1987, Paras Healthcare Limited operates eight hospitals under the “Paras Health” brand, with a combined capacity of 2,211 beds across North India, Bihar and Jharkhand. As of June 30, 2026, its hospital network covered five states and one Union Territory.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The company plans to add 300 beds in Gurugram by Fiscal 2027 and another 500 beds in Ludhiana by Fiscal 2028, taking its total capacity to 3,011 beds by March 2028. Its major specialties include cardiac sciences, oncology, neurosciences, gastro sciences, orthopaedics and renal sciences, which together accounted for 74.70% of its revenue in Fiscal 2026. As of June 30, 2026, the company employed 1,084 doctors and 1,937 nurses.</span></p>
