Fusion CX IPO Fully Subscribed 4.68x on Final Day

Updated At: 19 Oct 20:00

Fusion CX IPO

The ₹702.00 crore IPO of Fusion CX Ltd. closed on Oct 16, 2026, booked 4.68 times subscription on the last day. The company has received bids for 6.25 crore shares against the 1.34 crore shares offered.

Fusion CX IPO Subscription Status

The Retail Investors category subscribed 5.99 times, Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs) subscribed 4.73 times and Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) booked 4.21 times subscription.

In the NII category, small NIIs who bid for up to ₹10 Lakhs subscribed 4.75 times, while big NIIs who bid above ₹10 Lakhs subscribed 4.72 times.

About The IPO

The issue comprised of an Offer-for-Sale (OFS) of 69,89,618 shares, valued at ₹202.00 crore and a Fresh Issue of 1,73,01,038 shares worth ₹500.00 crore.

The allotment is likely to be finalized on Oct 19, 2026 and the shares are proposed to be listed on NSE and BSE with tentative listing date on Oct 22, 2026.

The issue is managed by Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd., serving as the Book-Running Lead Manager, and Kfin Technologies Ltd., acting as the Registrar for the offering.
