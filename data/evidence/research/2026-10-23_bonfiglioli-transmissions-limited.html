Bonfiglioli Transmissions IPO is a book build issue of ₹2,533.66 crores. The issue is entirely an offer for sale of 4.32 crore shares of ₹2,533.66 crore.

Bonfiglioli Transmissions IPO bidding opened for subscription on Oct 15, 2026 and will close on Oct 19, 2026. The allotment for the Bonfiglioli Transmissions IPO is expected to be finalized on Oct 21, 2026. Bonfiglioli Transmissions IPO will list on NSE and BSE with a tentative listing date fixed as Oct 23, 2026.

Bonfiglioli Transmissions IPO is set issue price band at ₹558 to ₹586 per share. The lot size for an application is 25 shares. The minimum amount of investment required by an individual investor (retail) is ₹14,650 (25 shares) (based on upper price).

The issue includes a reservation of up to 50,000 shares for employees offered at a discount of ₹29.00 to the issue price.

Axis Capital Ltd. is the book running lead manager and MUFG Intime India Pvt.Ltd. is the registrar of the issue.

Refer to Bonfiglioli Transmissions IPO RHP for detailed Information.

About Bonfiglioli Transmissions Ltd.

Updated on Oct 19, 2026

Bonfiglioli Transmissions Ltd is one of India’s leading technology-driven companies providing mechanical and electro-mechanical power transmission and industrial drive solutions. The company designs and manufactures high-precision, mission-critical gearboxes and drive systems that are used across a wide range of industries.

Product Portfolio: Its product portfolio includes low, medium and heavy duty gear motors and gearboxes in torque capacities ranging from 13 Nm to 1,400 kNm Helical, bevel, planetary and worm gearboxes and gear motors, electric motors; variable frequency drives, servo motors and drives, and precision drives for robotics & automation solutions.

The business serves three main market segments based on end-use applications, including Industry and Automation Solutions, Off-Highway Mobility, and Wind Industry. In the Industry and Automation Solutions segment, it is among the largest manufacturers of gearboxes and drives in India, serving both original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and end-users.

In India, it operates three manufacturing facilities—two in Tamil Nadu (Thirumudivakkam and Mannur) and one in Maharashtra (Talegaon). Together, these facilities produced over 341,133 gear motors and gearboxes in Fiscal 2025. As of June 30, 2026, we had 215 suppliers in India and 70 suppliers outside India.

The company also offers strong after-sales support through two dedicated channels. This includes a team of in-house service engineers based at our 13 sales offices across India, with three offices located at the manufacturing sites in Thirumudivakkam, Mannur, and Talegaon.

As of June 30, 2026, the company had 71 touchpoints for customer sales and service across India, including three manufacturing facilities, 13 sales offices, 13 authorized third-party service facilities and 44 authorized dealers with 54 outlets. As of June 30, 2026 the company a network of 285 suppliers of which 215 were located in India and 70 were located overseas including in Italy, China, USA and Germany.

As of June 30, 2026, the company had 1,032 personnel involved in various aspects of our operations, which includes onroll employees and contract workers.

Competitive Strengths:

  • End-to-end technology-led power transmissions and industrial drive solutions provider across business verticals.
  • Highly diversified offerings across business verticals and customer industries.
  • Established parentage and trusted brand backed by global and local R&D capabilities.
  • Deep relationships with marquee customers.
  • State-of-the-art manufacturing infrastructure.
  • Experienced management team delivering strong financial performance
