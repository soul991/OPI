Fusion CX IPO Opens Today: Everything Investors Need to Know

Updated At: 14 Oct 20:00

Fusion CX IPO

Fusion CX Ltd is aiming to raise ₹702.00 crore through its IPO, which opens for bidding today, Oct 14, 2026. The public issue will remain open for subscription until Oct 16, 2026.

Fusion CX IPO Detailed Insights

  • IPO Type: Mainboard IPO
  • Issue Size: ₹702.00 crore
  • Fresh Issue: 1,73,01,038 equity shares worth ₹500.00 crore
  • Offer for Sale: 69,89,619 equity shares worth ₹202.00 crore
  • Price Band : ₹275 to ₹289 per share

Issue Allocation

Out of 2,42,90,657 total shares offered, 24,29,066 shares are reserved for Retail Individual Investors (RIIs), 36,43,598 shares for Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs) and 1,82,17,992 shares for Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs).

IPO Minimum Investment

The book-built issue is priced in the range of ₹275 to ₹289 per share and the minimum lot size for an application is 51 shares.

Investors applying under the Retail Individual Investor (RII) category are required to make a minimum investment of ₹14,739, while the maximum investment allowed for retail investors is restricted to ₹2 lakh. while Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs) must invest atleast ₹2,06,346.

IPO Fund Utilization

The Company proposes to utilise the Net Proceeds from the Issue towards the following objects:

#Issue ObjectsEst Amt (₹ Cr.)
1Repayment/prepayment, in full or in part, of certain outstanding borrowings availed by (i) Company; (ii) certain Subsidiaries (direct and certain step-down subsidiaries)275.70
2Investment in step-down subsidiaries, Omind Technologies Inc. and Omind Technologies Pvt.Ltd., for upgrading IT tools i.e. Arya and MindVoice61.15
3Pursuing inorganic growth through unidentified acquisitions and other strategic initiatives and general corporate purposes
Total336.84

IPO Management and Listing

The IPO is managed by Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd., as the Book Running Lead Manager while Kfin Technologies Ltd. has been appointed as the official Registrar for the issue.

About Company

Incorporated in 2004, Fusion CX Limited is a customer experience (CX) and business process solutions provider offering multichannel customer support through voice, email, chat, social media and messaging. The company also provides AI data infrastructure services, including data collection, data annotation, image and video labelling, and robotic teleoperations.
