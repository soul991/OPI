Zetwerk IPO is a book build issue of ₹5,012.33 crores. The issue is a combination of fresh issue of 18.75 crore shares aggregating to ₹2,830.00 crores and offer for sale of 14.45 crore shares aggregating to ₹2,182.33 crores.

Zetwerk IPO bidding opened for subscription on Oct 15, 2026 and will close on Oct 19, 2026. The allotment for the Zetwerk IPO is expected to be finalized on Oct 21, 2026. Zetwerk IPO will list on NSE and BSE with a tentative listing date fixed as Oct 23, 2026.

Zetwerk IPO is set issue price band at ₹143 to ₹151 per share. The lot size for an application is 99 shares. The minimum amount of investment required by an individual investor (retail) is ₹14,949 (99 shares) (based on upper price).

The issue includes a reservation of up to 7,00,000 shares for employees offered at a discount of ₹14.00 to the issue price.

Kotak Mahindra Capital Co.Ltd. is the book running lead manager and Kfin Technologies Ltd. is the registrar of the issue.

Refer to Zetwerk IPO RHP for detailed Information.

About Zetwerk Manufacturing Business Ltd.

Updated on Oct 19, 2026

Incorporated in 2017, Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Private Limited is a technology-led global manufacturing platform. The Company operates as a managed marketplace connecting industrial and consumer enterprises with a global network of manufacturing partners for custom and large-scale production requirements. The company serves across sizes, from innovative startups to mature companies, and across industries, including utilities, renewables, consumer electronics, artificial intelligence infrastructure, aerospace, space & defence, oil & gas, and industrial automation.

The Company follows an asset-light business model and provides end-to-end solutions, including design, supplier matching, quality control, and logistics. The Company’s proprietary platform, Zetwerk OS, enables real-time tracking and transparency across the manufacturing lifecycle. Its key strengths include scalable manufacturing capacity, strong quality control systems, faster delivery timelines, and a diversified global customer base.

It operates across key sectors such as electronics, renewable energy, aerospace and defence, capital goods, and precision manufacturing. Zetwerk has a presence in over 25 countries and a network of 10,000+ suppliers and 1,100+ customers, including Fortune 500 companies. It also operates 20+ manufacturing facilities across India, the United States, and Mexico.

In Fiscal 2026, the company had 1,802 customers, comprising 1,613 customers within India and 189 customers outside India.

Competitive Strengths:

  • Unique business model enables us to serve multiple demand pools and makes them resilient across economic cycles
  • Building Capabilities to Serve Multiple Industry Verticals
  • Ability to scale in a capital-efficient manner
  • Zetwerk OS, their proprietary technology enables end‑to-end workflow management across the manufacturing lifecycle while driving powerful network effects across their operations
  • Deep relationships with customers driving higher revenue visibility and stickiness
  • Ability to add value-additive ecosystem services to the manufacturing platform
  • Experienced, cycle-tested management team backed by an experienced board of directors
