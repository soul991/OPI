About EAAA India Alternatives Ltd.

Updated on Oct 30, 2026

Incorporated on May 14, 2008, EAAA India Alternatives Limited is an alternative investment management platform focused on India’s real assets and private credit markets. It manages investment funds and provides alternative investment solutions to domestic and global institutional investors, high-net-worth individuals (“HNIs”), ultra-high-net-worth individuals (“UHNIs”) and family offices.The Company operates primarily through two business verticals: Real Assets and Private Credit, along with portfolio management services and other investment solutions. Its Real Assets vertical invests in infrastructure, commercial real estate, perpetual capital and energy transition opportunities through equity, quasi-equity and debt instruments. Its Private Credit vertical provides financing solutions across performing credit, special situations, real estate credit and investment-grade credit. The Company also offers co-investment opportunities through portfolio management services.

As of June 30, 2026, the Company managed assets under management (“AUM”) of ₹953,448.78 million and fee-paying assets under management (“FPAUM”) of ₹486,231.76 million. Its portfolio comprised 10 active Real Assets funds, including two Infrastructure Investment Trusts (“InvITs”), and 16 active Private Credit funds. The Company is supported by its wholly owned subsidiary, Sekura India Management Limited, which provides asset operating and management capabilities, including operations, maintenance, monitoring and efficiency improvement.

The Company has a diversified client base and a technology-enabled investment platform supported by dedicated investment, asset management, coverage and enterprise teams.

As of June 30, 2026, it had 277 employees across investment, asset operating and management, coverage, enterprise, legal and compliance, and other functions.

Competitive Strengths

  • Established Market Presence: Over 17 years of experience in alternative investment management.
  • Diversified Investment Platform: Presence across real assets, private credit and portfolio management services.
  • Strong Assets Under Management: Managed AUM of ₹953,448.78 million as of June 30, 2026.
  • Diversified Client Base: Long-standing relationships with institutional and non-institutional investors across multiple geographies.
  • Technology-Enabled Operations: Dedicated asset operating and management platform through Sekura India Management Limited.
  • Comprehensive Risk Management: Structured investment assessment, portfolio monitoring and risk management systems.
  • Experienced Management Team: Led by professionals with extensive asset management, investment and risk management experience.
