## 6. `phases/Phase 20/preregistration.md` (commit before Workstream A)
State, before any workstream code: (1) **Exchange policy:** an outcome is the first-session opening price on NSE for IPOs listed on NSE; for IPOs listed only on BSE it is the BSE opening price; NSE and BSE values are never mixed or averaged. If the Gate G6 finding shows the master dataset follows a different convention, write that convention instead and quote the evidence. (2) The 1.0% tolerance and the Phase 19 majority rule are unchanged; Yahoo and any Yahoo-derived page count as one publisher; values with `UNKNOWN` exchange or quantity are not compared. (3) No outcome, evidence file or log row is written by hand; every quoted path, run id, price and hash must exist in the repo or saved raw output. (4) The policy was written after the BLS Polymers (1.57%) and Bombay Coated divergences were already visible; any row resolved by it is tagged `tier2_arbitrated` and reported with and without. (5) n = 11 listed of 30: all metrics descriptive and provisional, no superiority claims. (6) `v4.0.0-champion` and `model/ipo_model.joblib` are not changed in Part 1; in Part 2 they change only by the promotion rule below.
(7) **Retraining and promotion policy (supersedes the "no automatic retraining or promotion" sentence of `docs/retraining_policy.md`, per the owner's instruction of 2026-11-07).** (a) Retraining runs monthly on all verified rows except `data/ipo_holdout_test.csv`, with the unchanged `model/train.py` recipe and hyper-parameters. (b) A new model is registered as `shadow`, never as champion. (c) Every new IPO is scored before listing by the champion and by each shadow model, and both predictions are saved before the outcome is known. (d) A shadow model may become champion only if ALL hold on paired prospective results: at least 20 listed IPOs scored by both models; the paired point estimate of MAE(candidate) minus MAE(champion) is below 0; the 95% paired bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples, seed 42) has an upper bound of at most +0.5 percentage points; the candidate's Brier score on those IPOs is not worse than the champion's by more than 0.005; no promotion within 60 days of the previous one. (e) Every decision, including KEEP and NOT_ENOUGH_PAIRS, is logged. (f) Accuracy figures are always reported per model version and never merged across versions; the official headline is the champion's. (g) The numbers in (d) are defaults chosen by the assistant for the owner's approval and are fixed from the moment they are committed in this preregistration; they cannot be changed after a decision is pending without an ERRATA and a new decision date. (h) With fewer than 30 listed IPOs in total all accuracy statements stay provisional.
